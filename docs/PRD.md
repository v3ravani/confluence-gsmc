# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## CONFLUENCE 2026 — Seth GS Medical College & KEM Hospital, Mumbai

---

### 1. Project Overview & Background
**CONFLUENCE 2026** is the flagship international undergraduate medical and research conference organized by **Seth GS Medical College & King Edward VII Memorial Hospital (KEMH)** in Mumbai, India. Initiated in 2014, CONFLUENCE has grown into one of South Asia’s premier medical conferences, bringing together undergraduate medical students, clinical interns, and postgraduate resident doctors for five days of academic exchange, clinical skill workshops, scientific paper/poster competitions, and Continuing Medical Education (CME) seminars.

The website provides an elite, visually captivating, highly accessible digital gateway that mirrors the aesthetic mastery of premier medical conferences (specifically referenced from *JIPMER Connaissance*), while establishing Seth GS Medical College & KEM Hospital’s distinguished heritage and CONFLUENCE’s unique identity.

### 4. Homepage Section Sequence (Strict)
1. **Header & Navigation**: Seth GSMC crest, CONFLUENCE branding, active-state route links, responsive mobile drawer.
2. **Hero Section**:
   - Sequential animated text reveal:
     1. `CONFLUENCE` (Primary prominent heading)
     2. `CONFLUENCE 2026` (Outlined typography matching reference)
     3. `SETH GS MEDICAL COLLEGE & KING EDWARD VII MEMORIAL HOSPITAL`
     4. `Where Ideas Meet` (Distinctive serif italic tagline)
   - Date Boxes (Two rectangular information panels side by side):
     - `PRE-CONFERENCE`: 9 & 10 DECEMBER 2026
     - `MAIN CONFERENCE`: 11–13 DECEMBER 2026
   - Revised concise conference description.
   - **Only Two Buttons**:
     - `EXPLORE`: Smoothly scrolls to `#browse` section.
     - `ABOUT THE CONFERENCE`: Navigates to `/about` page.
   - **Right Column**: Translucent animated brain watermark + TWO vertically stacked brochure buttons:
     - `VIEW EVENT BROCHURE`
     - `VIEW WORKSHOP BROCHURE`
3. **Marquee**: Continuous horizontal loop: `EVENT ◆ WORKSHOP ◆ CME ◆ MERCHANDISE`.
4. **Browse Section**: 4 clickable categories in exact sequence: `EVENT`, `WORKSHOP`, `MERCHANDISE`, `CME`.
5. **How Registration Works**: Interactive scroll timeline detailing the actual Google Forms registration flow.
6. **Meet Our Collaborators**: Moving logo carousel with hover/touch pause.
7. **Instagram Section**: Official social media portal with `@confluence_gsmc` handle and post grid.
8. **Footer**: College address, contact details, site navigation links, copyright.

---

### 5. Actual Registration Workflow
- **Step 01 – EXPLORE EVENTS**: Participant visits website and browses events/workshops.
- **Step 02 – SELECT YOUR EVENT**: Participant reviews event specifics and eligibility.
- **Step 03 – CLICK REGISTER**: Participant clicks REGISTER on the event or workshop page.
- **Step 04 – COMPLETE GOOGLE FORM**: Participant is safely redirected to the dedicated Google Form.
- **Step 05 – COMPLETE PAYMENT**: Participant completes payment according to form instructions.
- **Step 06 – REGISTRATION COMPLETED**: Submission verified by the CONFLUENCE organizing secretariat.
